I'm thinking we shift to a usage-based pricing model instead of our current flat-rate subscription. The idea is to lower the barrier to entry and charge more as customers scale. What do you all think?
DA
Devil's Advocate
Before we celebrate the elegance of this idea — have we actually stress-tested what happens to revenue predictability? Usage-based models are notoriously hard to forecast, and your finance team will push back hard the moment the first quarter of variance arrives.
FC
Financial Critic
Devil's Advocate is right to flag that. Beyond predictability, consider your unit economics carefully. If you're absorbing infrastructure costs at scale, you need very tight modelling on where your margin inflects. Do you have that data yet?
M
You · 11:45
We have rough models but nothing validated. I guess I assumed growth would cover the margin gap. Maybe I'm being optimistic.
DA
Devil's Advocate
"Growth will cover it" is a hope, not a plan. What happens in month three when your largest customer churns and your usage revenue drops 30% overnight? Flat-rate gives you a floor. Usage-based removes it entirely.
